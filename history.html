Track record

Real listing data, not cherry-picked — the wins and the misses both stay on the page.

How the historical call is worked out: going forward, a new IPO gets the full 16-factor read (financials, valuation, debt, promoters, and more). For IPOs that already listed, that kind of research isn't practical to redo after the fact for every name — so this page applies just the GMP-tier rule consistently: GMP ≥20% of issue price → Best, 10–20% → Good, 0–10% → Average, negative → Weak. "Held" checks whether the actual listing outcome matched what that tier would predict. Source: closing GMP and listing-day prices as reported by MrMoneyFrugal's monthly GMP-vs-listing studies, cross-checked against issue price.

GMP is an unofficial, unregulated indicator. Past accuracy of the GMP-tier rule is not a guarantee of future results — it's shown here so the logic behind this site's calls can be checked against reality, not taken on faith.
